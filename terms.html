2. Hallmarking guarantee

Every gold piece over 50 grams and every piece of gold above ₹150,000 is hallmarked as per the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) regulations. The hallmark number and the HUID are recorded in your name and handed over at billing. We will verify the hallmark free of charge at any time, or at any jeweller of your choosing.

We do not knowingly sell unstamped or falsely stamped gold. If any piece is found to lack a valid hallmark, we will refund it in full and pay the testing charges.

3. Diamond certification

Every natural or lab-grown diamond above 0.3 carat ships with an independent laboratory report from GIA or IGI. Lab-grown stones are permanently laser-inscribed and disclosed as such. We never present a lab-grown stone as natural.
