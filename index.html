Our story

We started in a 400 sq ft workshop in New Palasia with one bench and two bench hands.

Seventeen years later Jewellery still runs from that one bench, and not much has changed. Every stone still passes through our hands before it reaches yours, and every setting is still done under a loupe rather than a machine press.

We are not the cheapest on Mahatma Gandhi Road and we do not try to be. What we offer instead is a name, a hallmark certificate and a person who answers the phone when something needs fixing.

— Farah Qureshi, Founder

The craft

Six weeks, twenty-two hands

The part nobody photographs, and the only part that matters.

01

Sourcing

Stones bought only from traced suppliers. Anything we cannot verify, we do not buy.

02

Designing

Each piece drawn at full scale and costed before a single gram is melted.

03

Wax & Casting

Lost-wax casting in recycled gold, then filed and burnished by hand.

04

Setting

Stones set under magnification. A poorly seated stone is discarded, not hidden.

05

Hallmarking

BIS assayed and stamped at the Madhya Pradesh bureau before dispatch.

06

Polishing

Nine finishing stages until the gold reads as light rather than as metal.
