Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Jewellery ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, enquire about a product, or visit a showroom, and how we use and protect it. It is written to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 as applicable in India.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address and phone number, when you submit an enquiry or private-viewing request.
  • Your message, so we can prepare for your visit.
  • Details required to process a purchase, delivery address, and billing information.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type and referring page.
  • Interaction data, for example which pieces you viewed and which pages you visited.
We do not collect financial card numbers, Aadhaar, PAN copies, or biometric information through this website. Payments are processed on encrypted third-party payment gateways and we never receive or store your card number.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a showroom appointment.
  • To process orders, arrange delivery, and provide after-sales servicing.
  • To meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations under Indian law.
  • To send you product updates or offers, only if you have opted in. You may withdraw consent at any time.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Our logistics and courier partners, limited to what is needed for delivery.
  • Payment processors and our statutory auditors, where required.
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities, where we are legally obliged to disclose.

4. Cookies

This website uses only strictly necessary cookies for functionality, such as remembering a viewing preference. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

5. Data retention

Enquiry records are kept for up to 24 months. Purchase and invoice records are retained for 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Request erasure, where it is not required for legal record-keeping.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communication at any time.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@jewellery.example.in. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data security

We apply technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to our size, including HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls on customer records, and staff confidentiality obligations. No system is perfectly secure, but we will notify you and the relevant authority of any breach as required by law.

8. Grievance contact

For any privacy concern, write to privacy@jewellery.example.in or visit our showroom and ask for the store manager. We will acknowledge within 48 hours.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced on this page with a revised date. Continuing to use the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
